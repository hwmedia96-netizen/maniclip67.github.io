Clip Studio

Clip Studio is a private desktop tool used by the creator Maniclip67 (@maniclip67) to turn long videos and live streams into short vertical clips and publish them to the creator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What it does

It edits clips on the creator's own computer, adds captions and branding, and, when the creator chooses, uploads them to the creator's own social media accounts through the official TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
