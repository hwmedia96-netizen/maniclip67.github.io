Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Clip Studio ("the app") is a desktop tool operated by Maniclip67. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Data we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, open ID) to show which account is connected, and permission to upload videos you choose to post.
  • YouTube: your channel name and ID, and permission to upload videos you choose to post.

2. How it is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the computer running the app, encrypted with Windows data protection. We do not run servers that store your account data, and we do not sell, share or use it for advertising.

3. How it is used

Data is used only to upload the videos you select to your own accounts and to show their posting status.

4. Your choices

Disconnect an account in the app's Settings at any time, which deletes its token from the computer. You can also revoke access in your TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or your Google account (Security → Third-party access).

5. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
